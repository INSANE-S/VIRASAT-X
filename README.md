# VIRASAT-X   
VIRASATX combines classic property-trading board game mechanics with rich Indian history. Players step into the shoes of legendary Indian dynasties, manage historical regions, construct palaces, and roll the traditional Pasa (dice) to conquer the board.

✨ Features
Historical Dynasties: Play as legendary rulers including the Suryavanshi, Chandravanshi, Maurya Raj, and Chola King.Heritage Board Customization: Rename rulers and strategically "Construct Palaces" instead of traditional houses or hotels.
Cultural Landmarks: Buy, sell, and trade iconic Indian heritage sites and cities (such as Agra, Delhi, Jaipur, Amritsar, and Madurai).Traditional Mechanics: Features authentic thematic details like rolling a Pasa (traditional Indian dice) instead of standard dice, and strategic Chance/Community mechanics tailored to historical events.
